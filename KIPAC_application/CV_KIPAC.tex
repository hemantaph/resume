\section*{Research and scientific software}
\textbf{Gravitational-wave lensing statistics.} Lead developer of
\href{https://ler.hemantaph.com/}{\texttt{ler}}, a Monte Carlo framework for lensed and unlensed compact-binary populations and detectable rates, with Otto Akseli Hannuksela, Anupreeta More, David Keitel, Chris Van Den Broeck, and other collaborators. Implemented source and lens populations, strong-lensing selection, elliptical power-law lenses with external shear, cross-sections, optical depths, lens-equation solutions, and detector selection. Implemented propagation of population-hyperparameter and finite-sampling uncertainties; analysis and publication are ongoing. Working with \href{https://pure.ewha.ac.kr/en/persons/chunglee-kim/}{Chunglee Kim} on star-formation-informed merger rates.

\textbf{SNR and detection probability.} Developer of
\href{https://gwsnr.hemantaph.com/}{\texttt{gwsnr}}, in collaboration with Otto Akseli Hannuksela, using partial-scaling interpolation and parallel waveform inner products for signal-to-noise ratio calculations, with neural-network methods for detectability estimates. Maintain code, documentation, and user support for both packages.

\textbf{Eccentric waveform development and implementation.} With Achamveedu Gopakumar, Maria Haney, Tjonnie Li, and Srishti Tiwari, co-developed \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12697}{\texttt{TaylorF2Ecck}}, an analytic, frequency-domain post-Newtonian waveform for nonspinning, low-eccentricity inspirals, based on our earlier work, \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.07956}{Tiwari et al. (2019)}, and implemented it in a \texttt{LALSuite} fork. Conducted Bayesian analyses of GW170817 and GW190425 and injection studies of waveform accuracy. With Lalit Pathak and Achamveedu Gopakumar, investigated the degeneracy between \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21955}{eccentricity and line-of-sight acceleration in GW190814}.

\textbf{Multimessenger lensing.} With Anupreeta More, co-authored \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02536}{forecasts for lensed short gamma-ray bursts and binary-neutron-star GW counterparts}. In collaboration with Otto Akseli Hannuksela and \href{https://iphd.site.nthu.edu.tw/p/406-1222-87294,r5205.php?Lang=en}{Albert Kong}, developing Bayesian posterior-odds tests for multiple GRBs using sky-localisation and time-delay information.
