Research and scientific software
Gravitational-wave lensing statistics. Lead developer of ler, a Monte Carlo framework for lensed and
unlensed compact-binary populations and detectable rates, with Otto Akseli Hannuksela, Anupreeta More,
David Keitel, Chris Van Den Broeck, and other collaborators. Implemented source and lens populations,
strong-lensing selection, elliptical power-law lenses with external shear, cross-sections, optical depths,
lens-equation solutions, and detector selection. Implemented propagation of population-hyperparameter
and finite-sampling uncertainties; analysis and publication are ongoing. Working with Chunglee Kim on
star-formation-informed merger rates.
SNR and detection probability. Developer of gwsnr, in collaboration with Otto Akseli Hannuksela, using
partial-scaling interpolation and parallel waveform inner products for signal-to-noise ratio calculations, with
neural-network methods for detectability estimates. Maintain code, documentation, and user support for both
packages.
Eccentric waveform development and implementation. With Achamveedu Gopakumar, Maria Haney,
Tjonnie Li, and Srishti Tiwari, co-developed TaylorF2Ecck, an analytic, frequency-domain post-Newtonian
waveform for nonspinning, low-eccentricity inspirals, based on our earlier work, Tiwari et al. (2019), and
implemented it in a LALSuite fork. Conducted Bayesian analyses of GW170817 and GW190425 and
injection studies of waveform accuracy. With Lalit Pathak and Achamveedu Gopakumar, investigated the
degeneracy between eccentricity and line-of-sight acceleration in GW190814.
Multimessenger lensing. With Anupreeta More, co-authored forecasts for lensed short gamma-ray bursts
and binary-neutron-star GW counterparts. In collaboration with Otto Akseli Hannuksela and Albert Kong,
developing Bayesian posterior-odds tests for multiple GRBs using sky-localisation and time-delay information.

LVK/GRASP commitments and service
