Refereed publications
 1. H. Phurailatpam et al. (2026). Constraining initial orbital eccentricity of inspiral-dominated
    gravitational-wave events with an analytic approximant. Physical Review D 114(6), 063003.
    doi:10.1103/94fx-wnjx. arXiv:2508.12697.
    Contribution: Lead author; waveform implementation, data analysis, and interpretation of results.
    Achamveedu Gopakumar also contributed significantly to manuscript writing and interpretation of
    results.
 2. H. Phurailatpam et al. (2026). ler: LVK (LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA collaboration) event (compact-
    binary mergers) rate calculator and simulator. Journal of Open Source Software 11(124), 7420.
    doi:10.21105/joss.07420. arXiv:2407.07526.
    Contribution: Lead developer, project maintainer and leader, and manuscript writer.
 3. O. A. Hannuksela et al. (2026). Strong Gravitational-wave Lensing Posterior Odds. The Astrophysical
    Journal 1002(1), 42. doi:10.3847/1538-4357/ae5816. arXiv:2510.15463.
    Contribution: Provided lensing-population statistics for the rate and time-delay terms in posterior odds.
 4. L. C. Y. Ng et al. (2025). Uncovering faint lensed gravitational-wave signals and reprioritizing their
    follow-up analysis using galaxy lensing forecasts with detected counterparts. Monthly Notices of the
    Royal Astronomical Society 540(4), 2937–2951. doi:10.1093/mnras/staf874. arXiv:2403.16532.
    Contribution: Contributed lensing forecasts and mentored Leo C. Y. Ng during the project.
 5. A. More and H. Phurailatpam (2025). Gravitational lensing: towards combining the multi-messengers.
    Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society A 383(2295), 20240127. doi:10.1098/rsta.2024.0127.
    arXiv:2502.02536.
    Contribution: Developed detectability forecasts for lensed GW and GRB counterparts using ler and
    gwsnr.
 6. L. E. Uronen et al. (2025). Finding black holes: an unconventional multi-messenger. Philosophical Trans-
    actions of the Royal Society A 383(2294), 20240152. doi:10.1098/rsta.2024.0152. arXiv:2406.14257.
    Contribution: Provided early-PhD research guidance to Laura E. Uronen and co-authored the study.
 7. J. Janquart et al. (2025). What is the nature of GW230529? An exploration of the gravita-
    tional lensing hypothesis. Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society 537(2), 1001–1014.
    doi:10.1093/mnras/staf049. arXiv:2409.07298.
    Contribution: Provided lensing statistics for the binary-neutron-star interpretation of GW230529.
 8. I. Gupta et al. (2024). Characterizing gravitational wave detector networks: from A♯ to cosmic explorer.
    Classical and Quantum Gravity 41(24), 245001. doi:10.1088/1361-6382/ad7b99. arXiv:2307.10421.
    Contribution: Provided lensing statistics and forecasts for future detectors.

inspiral waveform implemented in a LALSuite fork; validation and Bayesian analysis: item 1.
