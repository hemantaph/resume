The lensing proposal distinguishes uncertainty in model choice and correlated population parameters, finite observed-catalogue statistics, and finite Monte Carlo sampling. These are propagated to rates and detectable distributions. SFR-informed fits to nearby LVK observations are not presented as unique determinations of high-redshift merger history. SNR-based detection probabilities are distinguished from calibrated search sensitivity. Null-evidence conclusions in the waveform studies are retained.

The proposed cosmography extension follows the population approach of [Maity et al., arXiv:2512.15168](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15168). It concerns the lensed-event fraction and time-delay distribution, with updated source/lens models, selection, and propagated uncertainties. It is a proposed robustness study, not a completed cosmological measurement or a claim to have introduced the method. This is distinct from modelling an individually identified electromagnetic lens system in the multimessenger project.

## Rebuild

From this directory, run

```sh
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error -outdir=build CV_CCA.tex Research_Statement_CCA.tex Cover_Letter_CCA.tex
cp build/CV_CCA.pdf build/Research_Statement_CCA.pdf build/Cover_Letter_CCA.pdf .
```

The `build/` directory contains compilation logs, extracted text, render previews, and the final verification report. Recheck pagination after editing, especially the first research page and the cover letter.
